_Rodeo_ is the greatest trap album of all time. Yes, I'm doubling down. Travi$ has something in all of his projects that's just unique to him. Just the way _Rodeo_ starts with _P\*rnography_ is so melancholic. The production and the storytelling style that starts here and ends with _Apple Pie_ just teleports you beyond your room. Even though Travis's production is very heavily inspired by Kanye, and Kanye produces a lot of Travis's songs, he's got his own touch to it, like I said before. Just watch [videos of him working on his albums](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XkwCnrZ0P4M) and you'll get it. There's one more thing too. I think the autotune just matches his voice so well, and so does the energy he brings to the studio.

Anyways, back to _Rodeo_. After the majestic intro, _Oh My Dis Side_ just slides in and takes off. The second half of this song is beautiful. 10/10. _3500_, Future goes off. A trap song with Travis and Future with a beat like this? You already know. _Wasted_, beautiful chorus and Travis's verse is very nice. Not a huge fan of Juicy J on this song. _90210_, one of Travis's best songs. The production, the flow switches, the melancholic tune, the hunger, everything just shows. At this point imagine you're in 2015, listening to Travis for the first time, halfway through his album, and you don't know who the fuck this guy is but you just know he'll make it. _Oh My Dis Side_ had you riled up and _90210_ convinced you completely. _Pray 4 Love_ is a very popular song. I like it. Not a huge huge fan but it's nice. Then comes _Nightcrawler_, one of my favourite songs to get me hyped up. Swae Lee might have some of the best vocals of all time, and they go so well with Travis's songs in general. I wasn't a huge fan of _Piss on Your Grave_ at first because I felt like the lyrics weren't fun to listen to (I usually don't even care), but later on I realized how good the fusion of rap and guitars was on this song. I'm yet to find another one with production like this. It feels like a pure Kanye and Mike Dean masterpiece. I love _Maria I'm Drunk_. What Justin Bieber did on this song just paved the way for future pop artists to hop on rap songs and just do what they do best. And Young Thug just does his thing. I love _Antidote_. This is the intro to the trap style Travis flaunts later on in his career. It's the type of song that goes triple platinum. _Sicko Mode_ wouldn't exist if _Antidote_ didn't. _Flying High_ is kind of an interlude-ish song I think, and it's great. Not _sdp interlude_ tier but great nonetheless. On _I Can Tell_ he just locks in so hard. The verse is so hard. And _Apple Pie_ might be one of my favourite songs in general and I think it's Travis's best storytelling. I just love the meaning behind it and the way it wraps up the album with the final bit of storytelling. The other two songs on the deluxe are pretty decent but I don't really like them, and I think that's because _Apple Pie_ puts a full stop to this album.

Do I do drugs? No. But I love psychedelic music. It's just beautiful. Feels like everyone puts more effort into psychedelic production to bring the atmosphere to you. Since I love going off track so much, my general recs for psychedelic Travis songs are _Coffee Bean_, _Sweet Sweet_, _Astrothunder_ and the second half of _Til Further Notice_. If you want to go deeper into the iceberg, try _The Curse_, _Grey_ and _First Take_. I'd say the whole of _Birds in the Trap Sing McKnight_ has a beautiful sound to it. I'm so conflicted writing this because I say that _Rodeo_ is the greatest trap album, but then there are days when I listen to _Birds_ from front to back and I can't help but think, do I like it more than _Rodeo_? I absolutely love _Astroworld_ and _Utopia_, and I'm not trying to sound superior by acting like older albums are always better, but _Birds_ and _Rodeo_ have a sound to them that I don't think can be replicated. _Rodeo_ is still my pick, but _Birds_ makes me question it every time.
